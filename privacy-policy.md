# Privacy Policy

## Overview

Down Below does not collect, store, or transmit personal data from players.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how information is handled.

## Data Collection

The game does not:

- Collect personal information
- Require user accounts
- Track user behavior
- Use analytics services
- Store cookies
- Transmit data to external servers

## Third-Party Services

Down Below may provide links to external websites and services, including Reddit, Discord, and YouTube.

These links are provided for community, communication, and promotional purposes. The game itself does not transmit personal data to these services.

When you follow a link to an external website or service, that service may collect, use, or process personal data in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms of service. The Licensor is not responsible for the privacy practices of external services.

If the game later integrates with third-party services that collect or process personal data, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Children’s Privacy

Down Below does not knowingly collect personal information from users of any age, including children under the age of 13.

## Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or stored by the game, no personal data is retained or processed by the game.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game’s features or data practices change.

The most current version will always be available at this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may contact the developer through the official community channels listed on the game’s Steam page.
